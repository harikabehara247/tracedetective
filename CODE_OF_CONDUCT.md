# Code of Conduct

TraceDetective is a welcoming project. Everyone who takes part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space, is expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Assume good intent, and give and accept constructive technical feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sharing other people's private information, and sustained disruption.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer, Harika Behara, through the contact listed on the repository profile. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove content, or restrict or ban participants who break this code.

This code of conduct is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
